About Me

Hello! I'm Mehdi, a passionate Data Scientist and AI Engineer with experience in transforming complex datasets into intelligent solutions. Currently pursuing my MS in Expert Big Data Engineering at Université de Technologie Troyes, I specialize in machine learning, deep learning, and advanced AI systems.

My expertise spans across developing RNN/LSTM models for predictive analytics, building RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems, and fine-tuning large language models for various applications. I have hands-on experience with modern AI frameworks and cloud deployment strategies.

When I'm not working with data, you can find me participating in hackathons (where I've won multiple first places!), contributing to open-source projects, or exploring the latest developments in AI and machine learning. I'm fluent in Arabic, French, and English, bringing a multicultural perspective to my work.
